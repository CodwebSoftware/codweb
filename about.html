What is Codweb and where is it based?

Our Legacy

Empowering Local Businesses Since 2018

Founded in the heart of Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar near the Seven Hills Flyover, Codweb started with a simple mission: to provide world-class software solutions to the small businesses and MSMEs of Marathwada.

What began as a passionate team in the Waluj industrial cluster has now grown into a full-service software house serving over 500 clients across Maharashtra's thriving economic corridors.
